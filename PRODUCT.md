# Product context

Written from the team's answers on 7 Nov 2026. Assumptions are marked.

- **Product:** Relief Grid, a simulation console showing aid vans that keep serving flooded households with no central dispatcher, and never serve a household twice.
- **Primary user:** hackathon judges (Neptune North engineers, overall judges, ElevenLabs and n8n judges) during a 2-minute demo, plus the team presenting.
- **Use scene:** a laptop or projector in a judging room. Seen from 2–4 m away, so sizes, contrast and colour coding must read at a distance. (Assumption: venue lighting is dim to normal.)
- **The one job:** let a judge break the radio (jam brush, blackout) and see, side by side, our vans keep serving with zero double serves while the central dispatcher stalls.
- **Surface mode:** Operate. A tool to drive live, with one strong visual identity.
- **Direction chosen by the team:** night operations. Dark slate ops room, amber vans, red jamming, radio links, split-flap counters. The team's earlier site Lanterne (cartographic plate, serif display, split-flap numerals) is the craft reference, not the look.
- **Must not look like:** a generic dark SaaS dashboard (near-black plus one neon accent, glass cards, gradient text).
