[Relief Grid] GreatUniHack 2026 · Neptune North[Video] 2-minute demo

Aid that keeps moving when the radio dies.

After Hurricane Maria, about 96% of Puerto Rico's cell sites went down. Relief work still assumes someone at a desk can reach every driver. When that link breaks, deliveries stop, or two crews turn up at the same door while the next street waits. Relief Grid is a fleet of six vans that keeps delivering with no dispatcher and no network, and never serves a home twice.

[01] How a van decides alone

  1. 01 · OwnershipAgree without talking

    Every van hashes each home against the vans it knows are alive. They all pick the same owner, without sending a message, and a van only serves homes it owns.

  2. 02 · GossipLists that only grow

    When two vans meet, they merge what they've served (a set union) and who's alive, and hand a home to a closer van that still has stock.

  3. 03 · SilenceSafe or Lifeline

    A van goes quiet: broken, or just out of range? Safe waits, so nothing is served twice. Lifeline hands its homes over and counts every double serve.

  4. 04 · Voice SOSHQ that can fail

    Families leave voice notes in their own language. Vans carry them to base, where n8n, ElevenLabs and Claude triage and reply. If HQ dies, vans keep the notes and retry.
